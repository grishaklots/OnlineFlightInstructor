Allocation rules

This instructor-only demo keeps everything on this device. New workspaces start empty; add your own students and landing windows. No account, server, or network connection is needed.

  1. Set demand. Add landing windows using DD/MM/YYYY and 24-hour From / To times. Changing From sets To one hour later; edit To for a different duration. To must be later than From on the same date. Open a calendar slot and click student chips to toggle upvotes. Highlighted chips with a check mark are upvoted; the Assigned badge identifies the current pilot independently of their upvote. Changes save immediately; the grid below is a read-only summary.
  2. Compute the displayed month. Automated assignments in that month are reset. Manual assignments remain locked and count toward each student's total.
  3. Give the next turn to the least allocated student with an available upvote. Ties favor fewer monthly upvotes, then stable student ID. Their earliest available upvoted slot is assigned; repeat until no demand can be fulfilled.
  4. Override any slot. Turn on Enable force assignment in the right-hand panel to unlock its controls and assign anyone on the roster, with or without an upvote. Turning this toggle off only locks the controls; it does not remove an existing assignment. An active manual override dims the upvote chips without erasing their selections; they remain editable.
  5. Clear assignments. Use Clear assignment beside Remove landing window to release an individual slot, regardless of the override toggle. The top-level CLEAR ASSIGNMENT button clears every assignment across all months, including manual overrides, after confirmation. Neither action removes students, landing windows, or upvotes.

Roster counts show allocated slots / monthly upvotes, not a quota. Manual overrides can exceed that denominator. The average uses all roster members; coverage alerts are signals, not proof that limited preferences caused an outcome.

Previously saved single-time slots receive a one-hour end time where valid. Slots that cannot fit that duration on the same date are marked for an explicit end time; their existing preferences and assignments are preserved.

Export data downloads a JSON backup of the entire workspace, including all months and the displayed month. Import data validates that file and asks you to confirm replacing the current workspace. Dates and times remain local, with no timezone conversion. Files stay on your device; nothing is uploaded.

Greedy equity is deterministic, not a guarantee of maximum slot utilization. Preference edits update demand immediately but do not change assignments until you compute or override. Separate tabs do not merge concurrent edits; reload before editing in another tab.
